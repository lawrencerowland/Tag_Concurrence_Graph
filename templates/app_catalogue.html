Source and refresh details

The generator counts each tag once per app and each pair once per app. The snapshot CSV and source checksum preserve the actual catalogue used. Its graph remains fixed when the original site adds or removes apps.

Maintainers can supply a new dated CSV to scripts/generate_catalogue.py and explicitly activate that snapshot. The build verifies the selected snapshot; it never fetches the live catalogue. Reproduce or refresh this view.
